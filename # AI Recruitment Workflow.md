# AI Recruitment Workflow

## 1. Client Requirement Collection

### Step 1: Initial Interaction

* Client chats with the AI recruitment bot.
* Client shares hiring requirements in any language.
* Bot interprets and structures the requirement.

### Step 2: Requirement Refinement

The bot collects additional details such as:

* Salary bracket
* Gender preference (if applicable)
* Skills required
* Location
* Age range
* Educational qualifications
* Experience requirements
* Any other hiring criteria

### Step 3: Requirement Confirmation

* Bot prepares sample candidate profiles.
* Client reviews sample profiles.
* Client can modify or add requirements.
* Requirement is finalized.

### Step 4: Candidate Matching

* AI identifies relevant candidates.
* Matching candidates are shared with the client.

---

## 2. Candidate Shortlisting Process

### Step 1: Client Feedback

* Client reviews suggested candidates.
* Client shortlists preferred candidates.

### Step 2: Candidate Outreach

For shortlisted candidates:

* Send email notifications.
* Send WhatsApp/SMS notifications.
* Publish the opportunity on the job portal.

### Step 3: Interview Scheduling

* Collect candidate confirmation.
* Schedule interviews based on client availability.
* Coordinate interview lineup.

### Step 4: Interview Process

* Conduct video interviews if requested by the client.
* Client evaluates shortlisted candidates.

### Step 5: Offer & Joining

* Share offer details with selected candidates.
* Obtain candidate confirmation.
* Share confirmation with the client.
* Schedule joining date.
* Conduct follow-ups.
* Provide regular status updates to the client.

---

# Candidate Workflow

## 1. Candidate Registration

### Step 1: Candidate Interaction

* Candidate chats with the AI bot.
* Bot asks about desired job opportunities.
* Candidate shares job preferences.

### Step 2: Resume Collection

* Bot requests the candidate's resume.
* Resume is analyzed automatically.
* Missing or important information is identified.

### Step 3: Resume Enhancement (Premium Service)

* AI can generate or improve resumes.
* Enhanced resume creation may be a chargeable service.

### Step 4: Candidate Database Creation

* Store candidate information in the JobClass portal.
* Create a searchable candidate profile.

---

## 2. Job Matching & Application

### Step 1: Job Recommendations

* AI recommends relevant job openings.
* Jobs are matched based on skills, experience, and preferences.

### Step 2: Candidate Application

* Candidate applies to suitable jobs.
* Resume is shared with the client through the JobClass portal.

### Step 3: Activity Tracking

* System tracks candidate activity.
* Notifications are sent for:

  * New matching jobs
  * Application status updates
  * Interview schedules
  * Recruitment progress

---

## 3. Additional Features

### AI Resume Builder

* Candidates can create resumes directly through the bot.
* Resume creation service can be monetized.

### Automated Portal Management

* Bot automatically captures candidate information.
* Data is added directly to the JobClass portal.
* Reduces manual data entry requirements.

### Notification Engine

* Real-time job alerts.
* Interview reminders.
* Application status notifications.
* Candidate engagement updates.

---

# End-to-End Workflow Summary

1. Client submits hiring requirements.
2. AI refines and confirms requirements.
3. AI identifies suitable candidates.
4. Client shortlists candidates.
5. AI coordinates interviews.
6. Client selects candidate.
7. Offer and joining process completed.
8. Candidate database continuously grows.
9. AI recommends jobs to candidates.
10. Automated notifications maintain engagement.
11. All candidate and job data are stored within the JobClass portal.
